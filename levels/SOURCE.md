## Level Source

`microban.xsb`: Collection of **Microban** (155 levels) by **David W. Skinner**, in XSB format.

The file indicates the following source: [http://www.abelmartin.com/rj/sokobanJS/Skinner/David%20W.%20Skinner%20-%20Sokoban.htm](http://www.abelmartin.com/rj/sokobanJS/Skinner/David%20W.%20Skinner%20-%20Sokoban.htm).

All levels belong to their original author. They are used here unmodified, solely to evaluate search algorithms for the academic COMP 3710 project at Thompson Rivers University (TRU).

The benchmark uses a fixed subset of these levels (see `benchmark.py`).
